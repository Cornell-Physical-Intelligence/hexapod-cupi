- An older acquisition arriving late can overwrite the newer held sample in `_DelayedStream.read`. This occurs under a legal custom jitter configuration; the nominal shipped cadence/jitter combinations do not themselves reorder. Add a per-row newest-acquisition condition with an explicit equal-time tie rule. Do not skip an entire batch when only one row has newer data.
- Persistent samples and pending validity masks created during inference can make a later selective reset outside inference raise PyTorch's in-place inference-tensor error. A map bridge also needs **every causally released frame**, not only the sensor API's final held sample. Add a versioned `drain(now)` seam for map fusion while retaining the existing latest-sample semantics for direct sensor observations.
