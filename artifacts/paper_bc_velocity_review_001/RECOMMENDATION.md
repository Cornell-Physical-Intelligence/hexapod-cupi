Corrections and limits to the verbatim Fable text:

- Loss magnitudes do not establish gradient dominance, and undetached routing is not known to produce the same solution. Measure gradient components rather than assume this. The predicted post-fit velocity loss is a hypothesis until the new fit exists.
- A minimum shifted-target error at zero lag is useful evidence but must not be a mandatory correctness test: physical history can predict or lag correlated motion. Exact data timestamp/endpoint reconstruction is the correctness check. Previous-action alignment uses the actual applied post-slew target, not necessarily the previous requested expert action.
- The default128 normalizer example is not fit002's configuration. For 3,760 BC rows and N environments, their nominal weight before rollout k is 3760/(3760+N+(k-1)*24N). At N=32 the weight before rollouts2/3 is about82.46%/70.57%; at N=128 it is54.02%/37.48%. Any future native batch must use its actual declared/admitted value.

- Missing native stop demonstrations are a real coverage gap, but stop failure remains informative and is not inevitable. Genuine accepted stopping trajectories may help a BC path; they are not logically necessary for all possible policies or for reward-based learning. Do not relabel moving observations to create them.
