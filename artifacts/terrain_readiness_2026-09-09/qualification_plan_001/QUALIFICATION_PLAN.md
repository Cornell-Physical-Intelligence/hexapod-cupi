# C-study Stage 3 qualification draft — 10 September 2026

**Reviewable proposal; not an admitted task, frozen acceptance decision, or completion claim.** This document defines the next terrain-locomotion foundation for the user-selected serial C study. Stage 2 remains a prerequisite. The separate physical program's G3 is the integrated polygon → navigation → learned gait → coverage mission; its physical four-bar G0 and hardware G4–G6 gates are not satisfied by this study. C-study Stage 3 corresponds most closely to a bounded simulation terrain foundation, with its observation interface and policy-in-loop fault behavior explicitly exercised.

`qualification_draft.json` is the machine-readable counterpart. `SOURCE_SHA256.json` records the source documents/code used. No GPU job, terrain generation, policy change, training task or main edit is made by this draft. Every numerical proposal below must be resolved and frozen with the evaluator before final held-out testing. Existing fixed gates are retained verbatim; a narrower command or terrain envelope requires an explicit versioned scope decision, not relabeling a failed release as complete.

## Declared terrain and command envelope

The proposed first release includes continuous rough ground, ramps, 10–20 mm steps, and 10–20 mm ridges. Continuous surfaces use the **measured triangle slope**, capped at 4.99° with margin below the proposed 5° bound; nominal ramp angles are insufficient. Steps/ridges keep explicit vertical faces. Original roughness can reach about 12.4° locally and original blended ramps 7.25°, so original admission is not evidence that their geometry meets the mild envelope.

Pits are an avoidance family only. Their 120 mm depth and approximately 240–440 mm openings are not footholds or crossings in this release. Pit floors remain real geometry while their openings are support-ineligible. Unknown/outside-course space is not zero-height support. Materials, payload and speed ranges are declared separately; this is nominal rigid-ground simulation until calibrated randomized ranges are admitted.

The existing mild curriculum proposes 0.05–0.12 m/s translation, up to 0.20 rad/s yaw, 25% flat replay and a 60-second course episode. Those are **unqualified proposals**, not mandatory capacities of the current 0.005 m/s reference screen. Keep low and upper evaluation speeds as unresolved parameters until Stage 2 supplies a feasible measured envelope. The final qualification manifest must contain actual numbers, controller state/schema, acceleration/jerk limits, payload, friction and timing. A script must reject null parameters rather than silently deriving a convenient easier test.

Root-link forward is body −Y, left +X, up +Z; commands remain `(v_forward, v_left, yaw_rate)`. Terrain course +X is separate. Record both body-relative bearing and terrain-relative travel direction. A scene rotation preserves their relative geometry and cannot substitute for uphill versus downhill, ascending versus descending a step, or cross-slope traversal. Qualify safe reset/support footprints independently for both sides. Current one-sided entry fixtures need additional start admission or new geometry before all these rows can execute.

## Proposed coverage and sample allocation

Use the same selected checkpoint for every row; no learning, adaptation of weights or evaluation-driven checkpoint selection during the final campaign. First run a cheap deterministic integration screen, then a small development/regression matrix. Only a promising frozen candidate receives the full final matrix. Screening is not qualification and its failures cannot be deleted.

This proposes **14,800 final walking episodes**, plus retained flat evaluation and separate 1,600 hazard/fault episodes below. It is a reviewable coverage budget, not an ETA or launch request. Each fixed-motion cell has 50 low-speed/rate and 50 upper-speed/rate episodes; score both strata independently as well as the 100-episode cell. A fixed-motion cell is not allowed to hide upper-speed failures behind its low-speed half. For ramps, steps and ridges, independently balance both crossing senses; continuous roughness balances terrain alignment. Publish the resulting smaller subgroup intervals without pretending every subgroup has 100 samples. If a claim requires ≥100 at each speed/crossing combination, increase allocation before testing instead of pooling.

Every cell uses 100 distinct reserved procedural layout seeds, with independently derived reset/dynamics/noise streams. `qualification_draft.json` contains proposed deterministic seed lists disjoint from current train/regression seeds; seeds are not yet geometry. The same layouts may be paired across command cells for efficient comparisons, but each cell's binomial estimate counts one independent episode per layout. Repeated clones of one layout are not 100 independent terrain trials. Geometry hashes must confirm actual distinctness and layout parameters must include more than reset jitter. These tests support unseen layouts from the declared procedural generators, not new terrain families, real sites or hardware.

The uninterrupted sequence covers stand → forward → strafe → reverse → opposite strafe, both turns, diagonal arcs of both yaw signs, gentle and tighter arcs, S-curvature reversal, fixed-heading bend, walk-to-stop, turn-to-stop and restart. Keep all terminal/failure events across the whole sequence; resetting between segments invalidates the transition claim. The continuous-twist cell samples intermediate bearings/curvatures within the admitted envelope and reports command coverage. Exact segment durations, curvature magnitudes, path lengths and deadlines remain mandatory pre-freeze decisions, with precomputed swept-envelope feasibility. Existing 3 m courses do not automatically contain every arc or long transition; use separately admitted bounded layouts or an atlas. Do not shorten motion after observing failures.

Every traversal must cross a declared obstacle/roughness region and reach its far-side goal with the entire robot footprint, then stop in a predeclared supported zone. Passing a short flat entry is insufficient. Reversed/cross-slope route geometry is explicitly distinct. A blocked command can count as correct hazard avoidance, but cannot count as a successful traversable-course episode. Timeouts count as failure unless the frozen case explicitly defines a safe-stop hazard outcome.

## Retained gates and numerical proposals

**Fixed existing requirements:** exact source/asset/checkpoint/calibration identity; current C-study 1.6 N·m applied actuator contract; current named joint/target/slew limits; no nonfinite observation/action/state; no unrecorded reset; full contact-data completeness; no introduced ground sealing or geometry distortion; existing Stage 2 static and transition gates; same-speed same-view smoothness comparison and quiet standing. Keep Benchmark 1 immutable. No mean reward, median success, attractive video, completed budget, early short screen or successful process exit closes the stage.

The current flat static evaluator retains zero terminations/truncations, nonfoot fraction ≤0.001, requested saturation fraction ≤0.005, planar error ≤max(0.025 m/s, 25% commanded speed), yaw error ≤max(0.06 rad/s, 25% commanded yaw), tilt RMS ≤5° and vertical velocity RMS ≤0.04 m/s. Existing flat transition and visual tests remain exact, including current executable implementation and source hash. Re-run the full suite after terrain training at unchanged flat commands; terrain-relative metrics do not replace flat tests.

The fixed quiet gates are planar excursion ≤10 mm, heading excursion ≤2°, worst-joint velocity RMS ≤0.03 rad/s, range ≤0.02 rad, target-step p95 ≤0.002 rad/20 ms, worst-joint requested saturation ≤0.005 and applied torque ≤1.60001 N·m, with zero terminal events anywhere in the trial. Use correctly converted SDK XYZW → explicit WXYZ telemetry. Require the existing ≥10-second contiguous scored quiet window; record and separately bound the latency from requested zero to that window. The acceptable terrain stop latency is a pre-freeze proposal, not invented after the rollout.

**Proposed terrain release gates:** ≥95% episode success in every terrain × motion cell and both speed/rate strata; report two-sided 95% Wilson intervals, exact numerators/denominators and worst cells. This is the prior plan's proposed point-success target, not a claim that the confidence lower bound exceeds 95%. Even 100/100 has a lower Wilson bound around 96.3%; 95/100 has a much lower bound. If a lower-confidence-bound criterion is required, set its larger sample/acceptance rule before the final test. No pooled all-family percentage can override a failing cell.

Add worst-joint requested saturation ≤0.005 and no nonfoot/base contact after the fixed settling interval per successful terrain episode; preserve all early failure/contact data. Requested torque is distinct from applied clipped torque. The reference-only screen's stricter instantaneous 1.6 N·m request stop is specific to that screen; it does not redefine the policy evaluator's existing saturation allowance. Exact floating-point applied-torque tolerance, terrain-relative tilt/height/clearance bounds, slip, energy, payload acceleration, path error, stop distance/latency and dynamics randomization ranges remain proposed/open. Define them from measured feasible controls and survey needs before qualification. Do not reuse world-Z height penalties on slopes or relax flat thresholds to admit terrain.

## Measurement and fault contract

Capture physical state before automatic reset at each 20 ms control, retaining every reset reason and episode identity. Capture motor demand/applied torque and violations at all eight 2.5 ms physics substeps or retain verified per-substep extrema/integrals; a single last-substep sample cannot establish a peak bound. Export runtime joint names and reorder by name. Log actual root-link pose/velocity consistently, COM velocity separately, raw SDK XYZW and correctly converted WXYZ, gyro/gravity, measured named foot transforms/velocities, distal/shaft/coxa/femur/base contact, contact validity and reaction/friction forces. Reject completed logs with truncated contact/friction buffers or missing measurement coverage.

Record raw requested, admitted and executed twist/targets, controller/reference state, clipping/governor events, residual/reference P/V/A and measured motion. A governor cannot silently transform a failed original command into a passing easier one. Compare independently measured root-link displacement with its velocity integral and desired course progress, with no reset discontinuities. Torque, success and motion metrics are per episode/joint/cell, not only all-motor averages. Positive mechanical work is the integral of `max(tau_applied * qdot, 0)`; report J/m only with nontrivial measured distance and report stationary work separately. Battery Wh/m is not inferable without an electrical model and remains unmeasured.

Terrain queries preserve pits, absent surfaces, out-of-course space, negative clearance and observation usability. Base height is relative to explicitly required eligible support samples; invalid/no support stays invalid rather than clamped. A mean support-height reference does not establish a feasible contact plane or force balance. Full pad/shaft/body swept collisions remain necessary; a single toe reference point is not a clearance certificate. Map/collision truth must agree within an explicitly calibrated tolerance.

The existing proposed map interface is gravity-aligned and body-centred, 2 × 2 m at 20 mm, four channels: relative height, usability, age and uncertainty. Its 0.25 s age and 15 mm standard-deviation thresholds are pilot proposals requiring calibrated errors. Preserve acquisition-time pose registration and a common monotonic timebase; unknown/stale/negative-variance/nonfinite/future samples never become usable. All required support and stopping-envelope cells must be usable. Never silently substitute exact terrain for missing sensor observations.

Propose eight independently reported hazards/faults: known pit, hidden pit/unknown corridor, stale map, excessive map uncertainty, whole-stream dropout, future/out-of-order timestamps, wrong frame/extrinsic, and finite-course boundary. Each receives 100 episodes for translational approach and 100 for rotating/curved swept-foot approach: **1,600** total. Require zero entering an unsafe/unknown support envelope, zero boundary/limit violation and a measured supported stop or permitted replan. Safe rejection before motion is valid for malformed-input cases; freezing physical pose is not. These finite trials do not prove zero risk. Detector/watchdog latency and stopping margins must be derived and fixed from actual measured stopping behavior.

Stage 3 may explicitly qualify a privileged terrain teacher plus a tested map-interface/supervisor foundation. That does not qualify a deployed sensor actor. Label every rollout `proprioception`, `ideal_teacher`, `corrupted_map`, `simulated_lidar`, `simulated_depth`, or `fused_simulated_map`; do not merge their success rates. A deployable student remains separately gated by matched teacher comparison, stale/dropout/pit response, actual moving-robot occlusion and timing. Owned Mid-360/D455 hardware, camera purchases, mount calibration and payload mass remain their own measured workstream. No ideal rays or synthetic replay establish calibration or onboard deadlines.
