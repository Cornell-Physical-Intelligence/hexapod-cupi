# Next terrain observation comparison

Build one small terrain task with three observation modes, sharing the robot, controller, physics, reset cases, reward and critic. This isolates whether failures come from locomotion, missing terrain information or imperfect acquisition. It is implementation preparation alongside Stage 2; the final selected Stage 2 policy/action contract and fresh admissions remain prerequisites for terrain PPO. No task, checkpoint or GPU job is created by this plan.

## Start from the evidence already available

The 48 `mild_curriculum_002` fixtures passed the actual fixture campaign: 3,888 ideal rays, 192 PhysX queries and 144 physical probes, with no reported contact truncation. There are 32 training and 16 regression fixtures across ramps, smooth roughness, steps and ridges. Their immutable original JSON still says runtime pending; the separate [terminal review](../../artifacts/terrain_readiness_2026-09-09/runtime/terrain_mild_fixture_001/actual_review/report.json) supplies the later admission. Do not rewrite old metadata. This qualifies meshes and probes, not robot traversal.

Continuous slopes are capped below 5°; step/ridge discontinuities are 10–20 mm. Existing original pit fixtures remain avoidance-only. No extra terrain generator is needed for the first comparison. Seeds 7103/8209 are regression data, not untouched final qualification. Keep all derivatives of one source seed in its existing split.

## Compare three modes without changing the plant

| Mode | Actor exteroception | Common critic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `blind` | No terrain packet information; zero latent, invalid map mask. IMU/encoder/action/command history only. | Same proprioceptive core, velocity truth and ideal terrain packet as the other modes. |
| `ideal_teacher` | Exact collision-derived terrain, labelled privileged. Geometry-hit, course-boundary and hazard masks remain explicit. | Same inputs and architecture. |
| `corrupted_map` | Acquired, delayed, masked/noisy terrain packet. No truth substitution for missing/stale cells; noise seeded independently from terrain/reset. | Same ideal inputs; these do not enter the actor. |

First use the same ideal body registration for all three and label it **oracle localization**. This separates exteroception quality from motion estimation. A subsequent separately labelled test replaces registration/IMU/encoder streams with causal estimated/noisy values in every relevant mode. Neither experiment proves deployed localization. No actor receives perfect root velocity, true friction or true contact force from its map packer.

Use the existing proposed 2 m × 2 m map at 20 mm with four channels: relative height, usable mask, age and standard deviation. `LocalHeightMap.local_patch(extent_m=2, resolution_m=.02)` yields **100 × 100 cell centres**, not the older 101-node geometry-grid convention. Freeze that shape, axes (rows forward, columns left), scaling and frame. Keep ideal geometry hazard/support eligibility separate from measured map usability: a visible pit floor is not eligible support, and a confirmed planted foot does not fill nearby unknown map cells.

A proposed 32-value encoded feature keeps actor arithmetic small. For a selected direct315 core this means a new 347-feature policy backbone and 350-feature critic backbone after encoding; raw map packets stay separately declared. Do not flatten 40,000 cells into five observation-history frames. Cache acquisition at its declared sensor cadence; retain current age at each 20 ms control. Put the encoder in the trained actor/critic module so gradients and checkpoint state are real. Use a zero-initialized added actor input projection and verify exact flat-action parity before training; normalizer, optimizer and rollout storage changes need their own shape/reset/reload tests. If another controller wins, rebind its actual schema instead of adopting these tentative widths.

Start corrupted-map ablation with timestamped geometry samples; it measures synthetic corruption tolerance. Then connect `Phase3SensorModel.capture_* / read / reset` and `PoseTimeline` + `transform_timed_points` / `unproject_depth` to `LocalHeightMap.integrate`. Explicitly distinguish synthetic corrupted map, simulated LiDAR, simulated depth and fused map. Mid-360/D455 prototype transforms and hypothetical D405 mounts are not approved C-study hardware mounts. The existing 250 ms lease is unchanged; a future-foot guarantee over a multi-second swing/stop needs actual reacquisition or supported abort, not an extended timestamp.

## Small sequence before allocating terrain learning

1. CPU parity: exact triangle/ray heights, rotated/translated course queries, unknown/pit/boundary cases, map time/shape/frame rules, full/mixed-row reset isolation and strict checkpoint reload. Resolve physics/action/source identity from the admitted Stage 2 candidate; defaults in `configure_omni` are not that contract.
2. Fresh 32 × 1,000 flat standing; then one full-C start/hold per selected family with pre-reset and 400 Hz evidence. Prove contact filters and queries agree on the actual collider. A short 2-environment translated atlas readback precedes 32 environments; do not blindly jump to thousands while joint-rate fidelity remains unresolved.
3. A small development comparison uses four fixtures (mild0 ramp/roughness, mild2 step/ridge; seed1103), two declared geometry-valid starts and eight motion types: forward, reverse, both strafes, both turns and two signed arcs. This is at most 64 paired cases per mode, 192 total. Start with the two continuous families. Pure turns require heading/limited drift, not nonzero translation. Route/speed/duration values remain mandatory prelaunch bindings to actual flat capability and course containment; different body heading does not prove descending terrain.
4. Train matched short pilots with identical update/sample budgets, seeds, reward, critic and physics. Proposed 25% flat replay retains the old curriculum intent. Report each mode/case, held-out regression and flat/quiet results; do not select a better-looking noise setting after viewing the final test. Before Stage 3 completion, use the separate frozen [qualification matrix](../../artifacts/terrain_readiness_2026-09-09/qualification_plan_001/QUALIFICATION_PLAN.md), with fresh final layouts and resolved numerical gates.

Keep the 1.6 N·m applied cap, selected target limits, original requested-demand rules, nonfoot/contact-completeness, pre-reset event accounting and quiet gates. Preserve 50 Hz legacy measures and 400 Hz angle/rate/torque diagnostics side by side; unresolved reported-rate bias makes mechanical energy estimates provisional. Record requested/admitted/executed commands, exact crossing/stop progress, source/asset/checkpoint/schema/reset/map hashes, all failures and matched real-time clips. Fixture admission, successful actor packet assembly or one terrain rollout cannot close Stage 2 or Stage 3.
